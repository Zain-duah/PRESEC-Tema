A SCHOOL WITH PURPOSE

Learning that
lights the way.

At PRESEC-TEMA, education is about more than what happens in a classroom. It’s a journey of curiosity, confidence and character.

Located in Community 11, Tema, our Presbyterian school community brings students together to learn, challenge themselves and prepare for the possibilities ahead. We believe every learner deserves the encouragement and foundation to make a difference.
